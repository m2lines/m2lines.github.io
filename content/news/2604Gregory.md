The sea ice emulator we featured a few months back, **FloeNet**, is now officially published in GRL. Led by **Will Gregory**, the graph neural network emulates GFDL's global sea ice model (SIS2) while conserving mass, reproducing sea ice and snow-on-ice trends and variability with volume anomaly correlations above 0.96 in the Antarctic and 0.76 in the Arctic across a range of forcing scenarios. [Read the paper](https://doi.org/10.1029/2026GL122981).
