This **Climate Week**, NYU's **Earth Systems Institute** is holding its [first public gathering](https://wp.nyu.edu/earthsys/events/), marking the launch of a new home for data-driven, AI-powered climate research at NYU. Led by Director Laure Zanna, the event brings together scientific talks spanning **ocean simulation, food security in the Sahel, energy infrastructure planning, and accessible climate information**, featuring speakers from **NYU, NVIDIA, and Schmidt Sciences**. The program closes with a reception to continue the conversation.



**If you’re interested in this event, please reach out at [earthsystems@nyu.edu](earthsystems@nyu.edu)**



To learn more about the Institute and its work, visit their [website](https://wp.nyu.edu/earthsys/).
