On September 21, NYU’s new **[Earth Systems Institute (ESI)](https://wp.nyu.edu/earthsys/)** made its public debut during Climate Week NYC. Launched by NYU's Office of Global Science and Technology, and **directed by Laure Zanna**, ESI brings together more than 40 scientists across Courant, Tandon, and Arts & Science to build data-driven models of the Earth system and put them in the hands of decision-makers. The afternoon featured talks on Samudra, our AI ocean emulator that can simulate 4,000 years of ocean in a single day on one GPU, the surprising greening of the Sahel, and how home electrification is reshaping peak power demand. A fellowship, a Distinguished Visitor Program, and a spring summit are planned for the year ahead.
